# Risk Analysis of the Transition from Student to Professional Life

A risk management study of what can go wrong when graduates move from university into their first jobs, and what they can do about it.

## Overview

The move from university to work brings career, social, psychological and economic risks at once. We treated it like a project risk problem, identified the risks, scored them and planned a response for each.

## What I did

- Identified 10 risks that graduates face when moving into professional life across career, social, psychological and economic factors, and rated each on 5 point probability and impact scales
- Prioritized the risks with a probability and impact matrix, where mismatched expectations scored highest at 25 out of 25, and set mitigate, transfer or accept strategies with concrete actions for each risk

## Methods

Risk identification, probability and impact scales, risk matrix, risk response planning.
